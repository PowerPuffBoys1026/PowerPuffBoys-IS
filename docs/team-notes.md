## Conflict drill (do this together, in class)

Two members deliberately edit **this same line** in different branches and both open
PRs. The first one merges. The second one then gets a conflict and has to resolve it.

Pick a line below, each member adds their entry on **their own branch** (not main):

- TODO: first member to claim this line
- TODO: second member to claim this line
- TODO: third member to claim this line

Then whoever merges second will see `<<<<<<< HEAD` markers here and must resolve them.
